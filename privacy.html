Privacy Policy

agente-videos · Last updated: 18 August 2026

agente-videos is a personal, single-user automation tool. It is operated by one individual creator to publish that same creator's own original videos to that same creator's own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

1. Information we collect

  • OAuth tokens. When the operator authorizes the tool with his own TikTok account, TikTok issues an access token and a refresh token. These are stored as encrypted repository secrets and are used solely to upload videos to that account.
  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): open id and display name, returned by TikTok during authorization and used only to confirm that the correct account is connected.
  • Video files and their captions, created by the operator, which are transmitted to TikTok for publication.

2. Information we do not collect

  • No data belonging to any other TikTok user.
  • No followers, comments, messages, analytics, or engagement data.
  • No email addresses, phone numbers, payment information, or location data.
  • No cookies, trackers, or advertising identifiers. This site sets none.

3. How information is used

The only purpose is to upload the operator's own videos to the operator's own accounts. Data is never sold, rented, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any model.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets in a private repository and are never written to logs or committed to source control. Video files are transient: they are generated during a workflow run and discarded afterwards.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the integration is in use. The operator can revoke access at any time from TikTok's own settings, under Security and login → Manage app permissions, which immediately invalidates the tokens.

6. Third-party services

Videos are transmitted to the platforms the operator publishes to: TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. Each platform's own privacy policy governs the data once it is received.

7. Children

This tool is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone other than its single operator.

8. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact
